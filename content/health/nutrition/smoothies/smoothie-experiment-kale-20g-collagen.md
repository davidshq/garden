## Original working data and uncertainties

The quantities suggest mixed preparation states: banana is recorded as "2 items",
while avocado (1 cup) and lemon (0.5 cups) use the author's volume convention for
blended ingredients. Those two are likely pre-blended; the banana retains a
whole-item count. This is less consistently a blended-volume recipe than the
[[health/nutrition/smoothies/smoothie-experiment-spinach-mushroom-boost|spinach and mushroom boost version]].

The column now labeled "Legacy ounces (unverified)" was originally headed
"Fluid Ounces". Its original entries are unchanged.

Inventory and readiness are undated working snapshots, not current stock.
IQScores and totals are retained as historical working data, not validated
nutrition or yield calculations. The added-water experiment explicitly attributed
its fluid-ounce estimates primarily to ChatGPT. The earlier table did not record
a source for its estimates.

[^kale]: The companion prose note allowed swapping kale with spinach, with 0.25 cups if frozen then blended. The applicability of this substitution to this table is unresolved.

[^stevia]: The companion prose note specified Thrive Market Organic Stevia with Inulin, rather than pure stevia; its sweetness rationale was attributed to ChatGPT and remains unverified.
