## Notes

Notes that are of interest to me, note that this is by no means comprehensive - effects I am not concerned about (e.g. blood thinning) are often not listed.
(1) Anti-cancer, heart health, allergy relief, healthy immune system, eye health (zeaxanthin), oral health, weight loss. Could worsen autoimmune diseases
(2) May help with upset stomach and nausea, decreasing pain during periods (but potentially increasing miscarriage risk). Helpful with osteoarthritis pain, possibly rheumatoid arthritis, muscle/joint pain, and headaches.
(3) 1 tsp provides 70% of Vitamin K. Anti-cancer, bone health, eye health (lutein, zeaxanthin). See also [MedicineNet's article on the nutrition facts of parsley](https://www.medicinenet.com/21_impressive_health_benefits_and_uses_of_parsley/article.htm) for larger servings - e.g. 30 g - where it begins to really pack a punch on the nutritional front.
