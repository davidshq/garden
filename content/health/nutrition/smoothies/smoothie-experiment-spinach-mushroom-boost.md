## Differences and uncertainties

The former linked table is a [[health/nutrition/smoothies/smoothie-experiment-kale-20g-collagen|separate kale experiment]]:
it has rainbow kale (0.5 cups), two bananas rather than one cup, two tablespoons
of honey rather than one, and no MUD/WTR Mushroom Boost. Neither record is treated
as a correction to the other. No added water quantity was specified here.

The original substitution note said kale could be swapped with spinach, but if
frozen then blended to expect 0.25 cups. This is an unverified preparation-specific
alternative, not an equivalence for the one cup of spinach above; see
[[health/nutrition/ingredients/spinach#Preparation and measured conversions|spinach preparation notes]].

The original ingredient label was "(Thrive diluted) Stevia". The stevia quantity
refers to Thrive Market Organic Stevia with Inulin. The
original rationale (attributed to ChatGPT) was that this blend contains little
actual stevia and around 2 g was needed to replace apple juice sweetness. This
has not been independently verified. IQScores in the working notes were described
as "totally off", with an intention to correct them in the future, and are not
used for totals.
