## Preparation context

This was the second trial in the original December 2025 section about
pre-blending ingredients. It follows
[[health/nutrition/smoothies/versions/smoothie-2025-12-simple-swap|Simple Swap]],
which explicitly describes replacing raw ingredients with pre-blended mixtures
and running out of space in the 64 oz blender.

Bananas (2 cups), avocado (0.5 cups), and lemon (0.5 cups) use volume rather
than whole-item counts. Together with the shared trial context and the author's
measurement convention, this identifies the recipe as using pre-blended produce.
The original notes did not identify the preparation state of every ingredient or
the water already included in each mixture. Keep the quantities below as recorded;
do not interpret them as equivalent amounts of raw produce.
