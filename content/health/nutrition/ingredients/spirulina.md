The original source notes mentioned anti-cancer effects, heart health, allergy
relief, immune health, eye health (zeaxanthin), oral health, and weight loss.
They also noted that spirulina could worsen autoimmune diseases. These are
preserved research notes, not verified conclusions or a comprehensive safety review.
