## Preparation and measured conversions

Undated observation moved from [[health/nutrition/smoothies/h-n-smoothie-maths|Smoothie Maths]]:

> 2x 13 oz frozen spinach (microwaved) + 4 cups water = 48 oz

The input and output ounces were not explicitly labeled as weight or fluid volume
in the original observation. The output was recorded in a blending-volume context.
Retain this as an observation, not a universal conversion; confirm the units before
using it to convert quantities. The note did not specify packaging.

The same note reported:

> I found 13 oz of frozen spinach that had been microwaved to still be a hefty load for my Vitamix - and I was parceling it in probably 1/4th at a time.

Water and spinach were divided over several blender runs.

The former "Next Smoothie Recipe" note suggested 0.25 cups of frozen then blended
spinach as a kale substitution. Its starting amount and water content were not
measured there. No measured conversion to one cup of raw spinach was recorded.

The [[health/nutrition/smoothies/versions/smoothie-2025-08|August 2025 snapshot]]
also recorded an estimate attributed to ChatGPT: one cup of spinach approximately
1.5 oz. The ounce type and preparation state were not specified; it is unverified.
