## Preparation and measured conversions

Undated observation moved from [[health/nutrition/smoothies/h-n-smoothie-maths|Smoothie Maths]]:

> 48 oz (3 lbs) of frozen blueberries (microwaved for 2 mins) + 2c water = 7.9 cups

The 48 oz (3 lbs) refers to weight; the output is cups of blended mixture. This
includes the added water; it is not a conversion for a cup of frozen or raw berries.
