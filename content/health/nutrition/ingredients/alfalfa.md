## Reason for deferring

Moved from the "Maybe Not" list in
[[health/nutrition/smoothies/h-n-potential-ingredients|Potential Smoothie Ingredients]].
The original concerns were needing to eat a substantial amount, difficulty
avoiding bacterial exposure, and apparently losing much of the benefit through
cooking. These are preserved research notes and have not been rechecked.
